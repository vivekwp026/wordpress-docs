Selective Synchronization via Bulk Actions

If you don't want to sync all records at once or wait for automatic synchronization, you can selectively sync specific items using native WooCommerce bulk actions.

This targeted approach is supported for Products, Categories, Attributes, Orders, and Users.

Users Selective Sync

  1. Navigate to the Users menu in WordPress.
  2. Select the specific users you want to sync.
  3. Select Sync to Bagisto from the bulk actions dropdown.
  4. Confirm your choice in the modal.
  5. A progress bar will track the upload.
  6. When finished, a success message will be shown.

Categories Selective Sync

  1. Navigate to Products > Categories in WooCommerce.
  2. Use the checkboxes to select the specific categories you want to sync.
  3. Click the Bulk actions dropdown, select Sync to Bagisto, and click Apply.
  4. A confirmation modal will appear allowing you to select whether to update existing records. Click Sync to Bagisto to proceed.
  5. A sync progress bar will appear at the bottom right of the screen.
  6. Once completed, you will receive a success message and the Bagisto ID will be updated.

Attributes Selective Sync

  1. Navigate to Products > Attributes in WooCommerce.
  2. Select the specific attributes you wish to synchronize.
  3. Choose Sync to Bagisto from the bulk actions menu and apply.
  4. Confirm your selection in the modal.
  5. The progress bar will indicate the sync status.
  6. A success message will confirm when the synchronization is complete.

Products Selective Sync

  1. Navigate to the respective WooCommerce listing page (e.g., Products).
  2. Use the checkboxes on the left to select the specific records you want to sync.
  3. Click the Bulk actions dropdown at the top of the table.
  4. Select Sync to Bagisto and click Apply.
  5. A confirmation modal will appear allowing you to select whether to update existing records. Click Sync to Bagisto to proceed.
  6. A sync progress bar will appear at the bottom right of the screen showing the synchronization status.
  7. Once completed, the selected records will be synchronized, and their respective Bagisto ID will be updated and displayed in the table column.

Orders Selective Sync

  1. Navigate to WooCommerce > Orders.
  2. Select the orders you wish to synchronize to Bagisto.
  3. Select Sync to Bagisto from the bulk actions and apply.
  4. A confirmation modal will be displayed.
  5. You can track the progress through the syncing bar.
  6. Upon completion, a success message will appear and the sync status will be updated.
