Synchronization

The Bulk Sync feature allows you to push existing WooCommerce data to Bagisto in batches. This is especially useful during the initial setup or if automatic synchronization was temporarily disabled.

Navigate to WC to Bagisto > Bulk Sync to access this functionality.

How Bulk Synchronization Works

The plugin uses a background processing queue to handle bulk synchronizations. This ensures that your server is not overwhelmed and the process completes successfully without timing out, even when syncing thousands of records.

Process Flow

  1. Select Entity: Choose what you want to sync (Categories, Products, Customers, Users, Orders, or Taxes).
  2. Filter/Exclude: (Optional) Provide specific IDs that you want to exclude from the sync process.
  3. Start Sync: Click the sync button. The plugin will fetch the relevant records and add them to the background queue.
  4. Background Processing: A cron job runs every 5 minutes to process the queued items in batches. You can monitor the progress through a live syncing bar in your dashboard.

Available Synchronization Options

Customer & User Synchronization

Sync your user base and customers.

  • Role Filtering: For users, you can include or exclude specific WordPress roles (e.g., exclude 'subscriber' if you only want to sync admins).
  • Default Values: Customers and users will inherit the default groups, roles, and statuses defined in the General Settings.

Category Synchronization

Synchronize your WooCommerce product categories to Bagisto.

  • Update Existing: You can choose whether to update categories that already exist in Bagisto or only create new ones.
  • Hierarchy: The plugin attempts to maintain the parent-child relationship of categories during synchronization.

Attribute Synchronization

Synchronize your WooCommerce global attributes to Bagisto.

  • Terms & Values: Ensures that all associated attribute terms are mapped properly.
  • Consistency: Maintains data parity for variable product generation between the stores.

Product Synchronization

Push your WooCommerce products to Bagisto catalog.

  • Supported Types: Simple and Variable products are supported.
  • Attributes: Product attributes, variations, and stock levels are included in the sync.
  • Exclusions: Enter specific Product IDs separated by commas to skip them during the sync.

Tax Synchronization

Push your WooCommerce tax classes and rates to Bagisto.

  • Tax Mapping: Uses the defaults defined in settings to correctly categorize the synchronized tax rates.
  • Automatic Rates: Syncs standard, reduced rate, and zero rate tax classes.

Order Synchronization

Push historical WooCommerce orders to Bagisto.

  • Orders are synced with their line items, totals, and customer references.
  • Prerequisite: Ensure that products and customers are synced before syncing orders to prevent missing references in Bagisto.

Selective Synchronization via Bulk Actions

If you don't want to sync all records at once or wait for automatic synchronization, you can selectively sync specific items using native WooCommerce bulk actions.

For detailed instructions on syncing Products, Categories, Attributes, Orders, and Users using bulk actions, please refer to the Selective Synchronization guide.

Automatic Synchronization

In addition to bulk syncing, the plugin supports real-time automatic synchronization. If enabled in the Settings, any new entity created or updated in WooCommerce will instantly be pushed to Bagisto.

[!IMPORTANT] Automatic synchronization relies on WooCommerce hooks. Ensure that your server can make fast external cURL requests, as this happens during the saving process.

Sync Completion & Notifications

Once a background bulk sync or selective sync completes, you will be notified directly in the admin interface.

As shown in the image above, a successful synchronization provides multiple indicators:

  • Success Toast: A message appears confirming the completion (e.g., "Product: Synchronization completed successfully.").
  • Progress Bar: The sync floating widget will show 100% completion.
  • Bagisto ID: The synchronized records in your WooCommerce listing will automatically update to display their corresponding Bagisto ID in a green badge.

You can verify the detailed results of each batch by checking the Logs.
