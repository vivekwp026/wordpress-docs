Overview of the Setup Flow

Seamless Design Integration

When you connect WooCommerce with Canva, you aren't just creating product images; you are empowering your store with real-time catalog syncing, professional design workflows, and seamless one-click image publishing. One integrated setup gives your store stunning, high-converting product imagery instantly.

Step-by-Step Flow

  1. Backend Setup: Ensure your WooCommerce store is running with HTTPS, an active license, and pretty permalinks (/%postname%/).
  2. Installation & Basic Setup: Download the plugin package, upload it to WordPress, activate it, and license your installation.
  3. Canva Developer Portal & Configuration: Create your Canva Developer App, generate OAuth 2.0 credentials (Client ID & Secret), upload the JavaScript bundle, configure WooCommerce settings, manage CORS headers, and configure permalinks.
  4. Using Canva with WooCommerce: Launch Canva from WooCommerce product edit screens or products list, access catalog & variations in the Webkul App sidebar, design visuals, and save directly to Featured Image or Variations.
