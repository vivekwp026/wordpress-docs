Sync Settings

Sync Settings decides how Printify products are imported, what Printify may change automatically and how orders move between the two platforms. The admin and each vendor have their own settings.

  • Admin: Marketplace → Printify Connect → Settings → Sync Settings
  • Vendor: My Account → Printify Connector → Settings → Sync Settings

Change what you need and select Save Sync Settings. The message Settings saved. confirms it. Hover the ⓘ icon next to any field for a short explanation.

Product Import Settings

Applied to every product imported from your Printify shop.

SettingDescriptionDefault
Default CategoryWooCommerce category given to imported productsNo category
Import Products AsDraft keeps products hidden until you review them. Publish puts them on sale at once.Draft
Import ImagesWith the product: each product arrives with its first five images and the rest follow in the background. In the background: products appear quickly and all images follow. Best for large imports.In the background

The setup wizard fills in Default Category and Import Products As with the values you chose there.

Auto Sync Settings

Choose what Printify keeps in step with your store on its own. These settings rely on the webhooks shown on Webhook Events.

SettingWhen enabledWhen disabledDefault
Auto Import ProductsA product created in Printify is imported and created in your store automaticallyThe product is still pulled in and waits on Imported Products for you to create itDisable
Auto Update ProductsWhen a product changes in Printify, the store copy is updated, replacing only the fields selected belowStore products stay as they are until you run Update from PrintifyDisable
Auto Delete ProductsA product deleted in Printify is moved to Trash in your store, where it can be restoredThe store copy is keptDisable

Select Fields to Update from Printify

Choose which product fields an update may replace. This applies both to automatic updates and to Update from Printify on the Imported Products screen.

FieldSelected by default
Product nameYes
DescriptionYes
PriceYes
WeightYes
VariationsNo
ImagesNo

Updated fields overwrite your edits. If you change a product's price in WooCommerce and Price is selected, the next update puts Printify's price back. Leave a field unselected, or exclude the product, to keep your own values.

Selecting Variations makes the product match Printify exactly, which permanently deletes variations Printify no longer has. If no field is selected, an update changes nothing.

Order Settings

Control how orders are sent to Printify and how their status comes back to your store.

SettingDescriptionDefault
Auto Export OrdersSends orders containing your Printify products to Printify as soon as they are placedDisable
Import Printify OrdersAdds orders placed directly in Printify to your store automatically as they arrive. When disabled you can still use Import from Printify on Manage Orders.Disable
Import Missing Products with OrdersWhen an imported order contains a product that is not in your store yet, the product is imported first and linked to the order. Excluded products are never imported. When disabled, the item appears by name only.Enable
Send Order Emails for Printify UpdatesSends the usual customer, vendor and store emails when Printify updates an order, for orders sent to Printify and orders imported from it. When disabled, no emails go out for Printify status changes, and none at all for imported orders. Emails for orders placed in your store and for changes you make yourself are always sent.Disable
Sync Order Status from PrintifyWhen Printify updates an order, the WooCommerce order moves to the status mapped below and gets a note with the Printify status and any tracking numberDisable

Order Status Mapping

When Sync Order Status from Printify is set to Enable, the Order Status Mapping table appears. Choose the WooCommerce status for each Printify status.

Printify statusDefault WooCommerce status

Don't change only adds an order note. Orders that also contain other sellers' items only get a note, whatever the mapping says.
