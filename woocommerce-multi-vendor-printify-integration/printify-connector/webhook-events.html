Webhook Events

Printify tells your store about product and order changes through webhooks. The connector registers them automatically when you connect a shop, and Webhook Events shows every update Printify has sent and what the store did with it.

  • Admin: Marketplace → Printify Connect → Webhook Events
  • Vendor: My Account → Printify Connector → Webhook Events

Live updates status

The card at the top shows one of these states:

HeadingMeaningWhat to do
Live updates are onPrintify tells the store straight away when a product is created, changed or deleted, and when an order changesNothing
Live updates are offPrintify is not sending updates yetSelect Register Webhook
Some newer updates are not switched onWebhooks are registered, but not for every update typeSelect Re-register Webhook
Could not check live updatesPrintify could not be asked just now. This does not mean updates are broken.Select Refresh in a moment

Webhook URL is the address Printify sends updates to. Use Copy if support asks for it.

Printify must be able to reach your site over the internet for live updates to work. A site on a local computer or behind a login wall cannot receive them.

Updates Printify sends

EventWhat the store does
Product CreatedPulls the product in; creates it when Auto Import Products is enabled
Product UpdatedUpdates the store product when Auto Update Products is enabled
Product DeletedMoves the store product to Trash when Auto Delete Products is enabled
Order CreatedImports the order when Import Printify Orders is enabled and it did not come from your store
Order Updated, Order Shipped, Order DeliveredAdds a note and updates the status when Sync Order Status from Printify is enabled; adds tracking for the customer when shipped

These switches are on Sync Settings.

The events list

The counters show Updates Received, Completed, Waiting and Failed. Use the filters All, Completed, Waiting, In Progress and Failed to narrow the table.

ColumnShows
Product / OrderThe product or order the update was about
EventFor example Product Updated or Order Shipped
StatusCompleted, Waiting, In Progress or Failed
DetailsA plain sentence explaining the result, for example Automatic updates were off when this arrived, so your store product was left unchanged.
ReceivedHow long ago the update arrived

Updates are processed in the background, usually within a minute. When Printify is busy, an update waits and is tried again. If Printify cannot be reached after several tries, use Update from Printify on Imported Products to fetch the product by hand.
